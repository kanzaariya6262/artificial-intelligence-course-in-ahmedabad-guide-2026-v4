Avoiding Overwhelm: How to Pace Your Learning

One of the most significant mistakes learners make is trying to consume too much information too quickly. The field of AI is vast, encompassing numerous sub-disciplines like natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics. Beginners often find themselves overwhelmed by the sheer volume of resources available, which can lead to burnout.

To avoid this pitfall, consider the following strategies:

    Set realistic learning goals based on your schedule.
    Break down topics into manageable segments.
    Focus on practical projects to reinforce theoretical concepts.
    Utilize platforms that provide structured learning paths.
